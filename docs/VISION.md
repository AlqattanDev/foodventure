# Foodventure

A tiny souq stall that makes people smile. Foodventure celebrates Bahraini and Khaliji food through playful, simple cooking: a stream of karak, a glossy swirl of halwa, a crisp samboosa and a plate passed to a happy guest.

A new player should understand by doing, enjoy their first dish within seconds and finish a short rush wanting one more. Future content should add distinct food interactions and charming visible rewards while preserving that speed and clarity.

README.md describes the shipped game. The old stock, payroll, pricing and expansion simulation is retired.
