# Foodventure status

The original 3D cooking/business simulation has been replaced by a lightweight illustrated souq cooking game. Current behaviour and commands are in README.md; design decisions are in PRODUCT.md and docs/REDO-DIRECTION.md.

The game contains an untimed first cup, 60-second rushes, five Khaliji dishes, two simple actions per dish, customer patience, quality and speed scoring, combos, three-star goals, recipe unlocks, visible cosmetic stall upgrades, remembered mute, local progress and guarded arcade submission.

The old tycoon save is preserved under its original key. The new game starts separate progression. See docs/VERIFICATION.md for checks actually performed and hardware limits.
