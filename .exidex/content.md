# Foodventure

A little taste of Bahrain: pour karak, fold samboosa and stir halwa at your own colourful souq stall. Make customers happy in joyful, one-minute cooking rushes, with simple touch and keyboard controls.

Build combos, collect stars and unlock balaleet, machboos and new stall decorations. The first cup teaches you by playing; each day offers another score to beat. Original illustrated food and a warm souq backdrop keep the game light and quick to open on a phone.
