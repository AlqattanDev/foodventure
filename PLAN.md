# Foodventure plan

The full redo replaces the earlier halwa mastery and business-management design. The current experience is defined by README.md, PRODUCT.md and docs/REDO-DIRECTION.md.

The playable goal is immediate: make food, serve a guest and see the score jump. A 60-second rush ends in stars and a next-day choice. Days 2–4 introduce the remaining recipes; later days offer a modest, capped pace increase. Cosmetics reward best stars without changing scoring.

The next product decision belongs to hands-on play: are the gestures satisfying and are the three-star targets worth chasing? Physical phone testing remains separate from automated browser emulation. Keep the recipe loop clear before adding new systems.
